Corporate Overview

Dedicated to Reliable Pharmaceutical Wholesale & Supply

Operating from Dir (U) Sundrwal Camp, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, K.G. Enterprises (Khanx Group) serves pharmacies, clinics, and healthcare providers with structured wholesale distribution across core pharmaceutical and medical device categories.
